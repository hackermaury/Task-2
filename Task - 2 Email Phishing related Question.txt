1. PHISHING EMAIL SAMPLE
--------------------------------------------------
From: support@netf1ix-billing-update.com
To: user@gmail.com
Subject: URGENT: Your Netflix Membership is On Hold!

Body:
Dear Valued Customer,
We were unable to process your latest monthly payment. As a result, your Netflix membership has been temporarily placed on hold.
Please update your billing information immediately within the next 24 hours to avoid permanent service cancellation.
[ Click Here to Update Payment Details ] -> (https://malicious-fake-netflix-login.example/update)
Thank you,
Netflix Billing Support Team


2. VULNERABILITY NOTES (SHORT & STUDENT TYPE)
--------------------------------------------------
* Fake Sender Domain (Typo squatting): Letter 'l' in Netflix is replaced with number '1' (netf1ix).
* Generic Greeting: Uses "Dear Valued Customer" instead of the actual name.
* Urgency & Fear Tactics: Threatening words like "URGENT" and "within 24 hours" to create panic.
* Suspicious Link: Redirects to an unverified, external malicious site instead of the official domain.
* Generic Signature: No corporate contact details or verified employee name.


3. INTERVIEW Q&A (SHORT & STUDENT TYPE)
--------------------------------------------------
Q1. What is phishing?
- Fake messages mimicking trusted entities to steal sensitive info like passwords and bank details.

Q2. How to identify a phishing email?
- Check for fake domains, urgent/threatening language, generic greetings, and suspicious links.

Q3. What is email spoofing?
- Forging the sender header to make an email look like it came from a trusted, legitimate source.

Q4. Why are phishing emails dangerous?
- Cause credential theft, financial loss, malware infections, and company data breaches.

Q5. How can you verify the sender’s authenticity?
- Check email headers using authentication protocols: SPF, DKIM, and DMARC.

Q6. What tools can analyze email headers?
- Google Admin Toolbox Message header, MX Toolbox, and Phishing Tool.

Q7. What actions should be taken on suspected phishing emails?
- Do not click links, avoid downloading attachments, report to IT security, and delete the email.

Q8. How do attackers use social engineering in phishing?
- Exploit human psychology (fear, panic, urgency) to trick victims into giving away data.
